Privacy Policy

Interplanetary Fund — Last updated: August 7, 2026

Interplanetary Fund is an AI-powered fundraising platform that unifies crowdfunding campaigns across multiple platforms. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. This is used to identify you as a campaign creator or donor.

Campaign Data: Campaign titles, stories, goals, images, and donation records you create are stored securely in our cloud database (Convex).

Donation Records: Donation amounts, donor names (optional), and messages are recorded for transparency. We do not store payment card details — all transactions are processed by PayPal or CashApp.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

1. Create and manage your fundraising campaigns
2. Track donations and display campaign progress
3. Generate AI-powered campaign content and outreach posts
4. Send campaign updates and notifications
5. Process payout requests (via PayPal, CashApp, or bank transfer)

Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest (Convex cloud storage). We do not share your data with third parties. Payment processing is handled entirely by PayPal and CashApp — we never see or store your payment card information.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can request account deletion at any time by contacting interplanetarysister@gmail.com. Campaign data may be retained for transparency purposes even after account deletion.

Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

1. PayPal (donation processing)
2. CashApp (donation processing)
3. Convex (cloud database)
4. Vercel (web hosting)
5. GitHub (source code hosting)

Children's Privacy

Interplanetary Fund is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us at interplanetarysister@gmail.com
