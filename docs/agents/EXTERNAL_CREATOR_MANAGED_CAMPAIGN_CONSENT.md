# External fundraiser creator permission and IFund-managed campaign workflow

**Purpose:** let IFund agents approach a fundraiser creator who is not an IFund member, ask permission to help, and offer either self-service signup or an explicitly authorized no-login managed service.

## Conversation sequence

1. **Identify and approach the creator.** Agents may research external fundraising opportunities and contact creators using already-authorized outreach channels. Identify IFund honestly. Do not imply an existing relationship, endorsement, partnership, or campaign approval.
2. **Ask permission to help.** Ask whether the creator is interested in IFund helping with their fundraiser. Before a yes, only give a neutral explanation and request permission to continue. Do not create campaign-specific fundraising copy, edit their fundraiser, contact donors about it, publish, or manage it.
3. **Offer exactly two paths after interest is expressed:**
   - **Creator account:** invite them to create an IFund account and create/import/link the campaign. They remain the campaign owner and can use the normal IFund agents.
   - **IFund-managed service without an IFund login:** ask whether they expressly authorize IFund to prepare and manage a campaign on their behalf. They do not need an IFund login, but must complete a secure, verifiable authorization and identity/beneficiary checks.
4. **Capture a separate mandate.** A generic positive reply or a reply to the initial invitation is not enough. A trusted verification step must bind the creator to the exact fundraiser and the named IFund admin account (including its stable account ID) receiving the mandate. The authorization must prominently state that this assigned admin account receives full operational control of that fundraiser for the mandate term, and that every AI feature and agent available to that account receives unlimited permission in scope to access and use the fundraiser's information and perform all available campaign-related AI work on the creator's behalf, including research, planning, drafting, editing, design, media generation, optimization, scheduling, coordination, analytics, and management, without a feature-specific or task-count restriction imposed by the mandate. Actual usage remains subject to the assigned admin account's subscription and usage entitlements, provider/API capability and capacity, applicable law, platform rules, security controls, and safety restrictions; this does not promise unlimited compute or free usage. Record the authorization version, identity/authority evidence, source URL/provider, duration/expiry, contact/delivery destination, review and revocation method, and the creator's explicit acceptance. Separately disclose and capture affirmative permission for publication, external posting/outreach, donor messaging, account connections, financial/payout actions, and payout-destination changes. No high-impact scope is inferred from the general AI delegation.
5. **Create a private IFund draft only after verification.** The private draft must identify the real creator and beneficiary, preserve source provenance, and have an audit trail. It is not yet a canonical campaign and must not falsely show the creator as an IFund member or claim they personally authored/approved content.
6. **Send the work back to the creator.** Provide a secure, expiring review link or other verified delivery path. The creator can review the complete title, summary, story, category and goal, request edits, approve or reject it, and revoke the mandate. Do not publish merely because the creator authorized preparation/management.
7. **Create the canonical IFund draft only after creator approval.** The assigned admin creates the campaign through the normal owner-RLS path, so the canonical `owner_id` is the assigned admin account. A separate managed-campaign profile identifies the creator and beneficiary and discloses IFund management. Later content revisions must again be reviewed and approved by the creator; direct edits that differ from the approved revision are rejected and audited. The campaign remains a draft.
8. **Publication and fundraising are separate gates.** Managed campaign activation is currently blocked by a database trigger. Do not remove that block until the beneficiary payout model, verified payout destination, separate creator publication/outreach authorization, and applicable legal/platform checks are implemented and tested. Donations must go only to the authorized checkout and payout beneficiary. IFund must not collect and forward outside donations or claim custody/authority without a separately approved compliant payment arrangement.

## Authorization states

`discovered -> invitation_sent -> interested -> awaiting_choice -> account_signup`

or

`discovered -> invitation_sent -> interested -> awaiting_choice -> mandate_pending -> identity_pending -> mandate_verified -> draft_preparation -> creator_review -> changes_requested | creator_approved -> publication_eligible`

Any state may transition to `declined`, `expired`, or `revoked`. A decline, expiry, failed identity check, or revocation stops campaign-support actions. A new mandate is required to expand scope or resume after revocation.

## Required backend model

Do not store this as an ordinary owner approval, agent delegation, social-account connection, or an unverified external campaign link. Implement a dedicated append-only creator mandate/audit model with:
- opaque mandate ID and one-time, hashed, expiring verification token;
- creator name and minimum contact/identity evidence needed for verification, stored privately with retention controls;
- source fundraiser URL/provider and verified control/authority evidence;
- named IFund admin account and stable account ID receiving full operational control; explicit acceptance of broad access by all AI features/agents available to that account for the identified fundraiser; separately authorized publication, external-channel, donor-messaging, account-connection, and financial/payout scopes; authorization text/version, consent timestamp, expiry, status and revocation event;
- a separate IFund-managed campaign representation linked to the canonical IFund campaign, with creator/beneficiary attribution and a clear IFund management principal;
- immutable event history for invitation, consent, identity verification, draft creation/revisions, creator delivery, review, approval, publication and revocation;
- creator-facing secure review/delivery access that does not require an IFund account but cannot be guessed, reused after expiry, or expose another creator's data.

**Managed ownership model:** canonical `ifund_campaigns.owner_id` remains the assigned, verified IFund admin account, preserving existing `owner_id = auth.uid()` RLS and owner-only campaign RPCs. A separate `ifund_managed_campaign_profiles` record attributes the real creator and beneficiary and discloses IFund management; no external creator user is fabricated and no creator account is created. The linkage function only accepts a draft owned by the assigned admin whose title, summary, story, category and fundraising goal exactly match the creator-approved draft. The linked canonical campaign remains unpublished. A database trigger blocks activation until a compliant beneficiary payout model and separate creator publication authorization are independently implemented and verified. Do not remove this gate or weaken RLS to make publication appear to work.

## Direct-to-provider destination workflow implementation (branch only)

The branch now contains the end-to-end application flow for requesting and approving an external donation destination, while preserving the existing managed-campaign publication block:

- The assigned super-admin can submit a provider label, exact HTTPS donation URL, destination account owner and relationship. Creation requires an active assigned mandate, a linked canonical IFund campaign, creator approval of the campaign draft, and the separately selected `publish` scope.
- The creator receives a high-entropy, one-time token link. The token is stored only as a SHA-256 hash. The creator review page shows the exact URL and destination owner and requires an explicit approval action.
- The approval endpoint rechecks mandate status, expiry and publish scope. It stores a SHA-256 hash of the exact approved URL, consumes the token, and advances the destination to provider verification.
- The assigned super-admin records evidence of provider-account ownership verification, then separately records compliance review evidence. A destination can reach `ready` only after both checks and creator approval.
- The database trigger locks the mandate row while destination records change, rechecks active/expiry/publish authorization, prevents mutation of the creator-approved URL, and allows safe revocation after mandate expiry/revocation.
- Draft resubmission, creator change requests, creator revocation and admin revocation invalidate outstanding destinations. The public campaign still cannot publish and no external donation CTA is enabled by this workflow.
- Provider verification and compliance are manual evidence attestations by the assigned admin in this branch; this does not claim an automated provider API verification or independent third-party compliance decision.
- Admin and creator UI, API client, Edge Function actions and contract tests are wired together in source.

The migration and Edge Function changes are **not deployed**. The branch's Edge Function currently running in production remains the earlier version. The new migration must be validated against the project schema before applying. The public publication gate remains intentionally blocking, so a `ready` destination is not yet a public donation route.
