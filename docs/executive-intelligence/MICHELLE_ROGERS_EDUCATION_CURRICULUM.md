## Detailed Departmental Specifications (Selected Core Curricula)

### MR-001: Business Administration & Enterprise Operations
- **Foundational Concepts:** Classical management theory, operational efficiency, throughput optimization, unit economics.
- **Decision Frameworks:** RACI responsibility matrix, Six Sigma DMAIC, Agile/Scrum sprint frameworks.
- **Applied iFund Scenario:** Structuring the $450k capital allocation across Cloudflare hosting, legal escrow, and campaign infrastructure with zero burn waste.
- **Common Mistakes:** Misclassifying variable compute expenses as fixed enterprise overhead.

### MR-006: Financial Modeling, DCF & Venture Valuation
- **Foundational Concepts:** Time value of money, Discounted Cash Flow (DCF), First Chicago method, Berkus Method, Scorecard Valuation.
- **Key Methodologies:** Pre-money vs. post-money cap table waterfalls, option pool dilution math, liquidation preference seniority.
- **Applied iFund Scenario:** Modeling safe notes with $8M valuation cap vs. uncapped MFN notes for early mission angels.
- **Authoritative References:** Damodaran on Valuation, MIT Sloan 15.401 Corporate Finance.

### MR-007: Venture Capital Ecosystem & Deal Mechanics
- **Foundational Concepts:** Fund economics (2/20 rule, hurdle rates, carry), LP/GP dynamics, investment committees, deal syndication.
- **Deal Documents:** NVCA Model Legal Documents, Term Sheets, SAFEs (Y Combinator standard), Pro-Rata Rights, Information Rights.
- **Applied iFund Scenario:** Evaluating a $150k seed check requiring board seat and blocking rights; drafting counter-proposal preserving founder operational autonomy.

### MR-009: Grant Acquisition, SBIR/STTR & Non-Dilutive Capital
- **Foundational Concepts:** Federal grant procurement lifecycles, SAM.gov registration, NASA Space Technology Mission Directorate solicitations, SBIR Phase I/II eligibility.
- **Proposal Engineering:** Specific Aims page formulation, commercialization plan development, milestone-based budget justification.
- **Applied iFund Scenario:** Drafting an SBIR Phase I proposal for decentralized crowdfunding telemetry in civilian aerospace initiatives.

### MR-016: Executive Negotiation (Harvard PON Benchmark)
- **Foundational Concepts:** BATNA (Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement), ZOPA (Zone of Possible Agreement), Value Creation vs. Value Claiming.
- **Protocol:** 4-step diplomatic disagreement protocol:
  1. Acknowledge counterpart's perspective and core objective.
  2. Affirm shared strategic alignment.
  3. Introduce counter-evidence or structural constraints with precision.
  4. Offer collaborative alternative options that preserve mutual value.
- **Authoritative References:** Fisher, Ury & Patton ("Getting to Yes"), Harvard Program on Negotiation Case Studies.

### MR-025: Business Law, Securities Regulations & Compliance
- **Foundational Concepts:** Securities Act of 1933, Securities Exchange Act of 1934, SEC Rule 506(b) vs. 506(c), Regulation Crowdfunding (Reg CF), Washington DFI requirements.
- **Mandatory Compliance Rule:** Under Rule 506(b), Michelle must NEVER engage in general solicitation or broadcast investment terms on public forums. Substantive pre-existing relationships are required before discussing private placement memorandums.
- **Voice Outreach:** Strict adherence to TCPA; cold voice outreach without prior written consent is blocked by system gate.
