## 1. Compliance & Ingestion Principles

1. **Public Domain vs. Commercial Rights:** Public accessibility online does not grant commercial exploitation rights. All materials ingested or synthesized for Michelle's educational departments are vetted for commercial training and operational reuse.
2. **No Scraping of Gated Content:** Paywalled publications (HBR case studies, Gartner reports, proprietary textbook publishers) are referenced conceptually under Fair Use for citation, with all synthesized lessons being **original authored derivative works**.
3. **Open Access Seed Sources:** Curricula are anchored in verified Open Access and Open Educational Resources (OER) repositories.
