# INTERPLANETARY FUND — PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** August 3, 2026
**Owner:** Michelle Rogers

## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1. Account Information: Email address, name, and password (encrypted).
1.2. Campaign Information: Campaign titles, stories, images, goals.
1.3. Payment Information: Transaction amounts and payment method identifiers.
We do NOT store full credit card numbers or banking credentials.
1.4. Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and
interaction patterns.

## 2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

- To process donations and route funds to campaign organizers
- To manage outreach across third-party platforms (Facebook, email, etc.)
- To communicate with users about campaigns and donations
- To improve the platform and prevent fraud

## 3. INFORMATION SHARING

We do NOT sell user data. We share information only with:
- Payment processors (PayPal, CashApp) to complete transactions
- Third-party platforms where campaigns are posted (Facebook, GoFundMe, etc.)
- Email forwarding to campaign organizers for inbox management
- Legal authorities if required by law

## 4. DATA RETENTION

Campaign and donation records are retained for a minimum of 7 years for
tax and legal compliance. Users may request deletion of personal data
subject to legal retention requirements.

## 5. DATA SECURITY

We use encryption for passwords and sensitive data. Payment processing is
handled by PCI-compliant third-party providers. However, no system is 100%
secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute data security.
