## Full Description (4000 char max)

Interplanetary Fund is your interplanetary fundraising command center — an AI-powered platform that unifies crowdfunding campaigns across every major platform into one cosmic dashboard.

🚀 LAUNCH MISSIONS
Create fundraising campaigns with AI-generated stories, press releases, and platform-optimized posts. Our AI Campaign Wizard writes your story, generates cover images, and formats content for every platform — all in one flow.

🌍 UNIFIED PLATFORM CONTROL
Connect GoFundMe, Kickstarter, Patreon, Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi, Spotfund, FundRazr, GiveSendGo, Indiegogo, Facebook, Bluesky, and more. Monitor every platform from a single dashboard. Publish to all platforms with one click.

💫 FUEL THE MISSION
Accept donations via PayPal and CashApp. Track every dollar in real-time. Automatic fee calculation, treasury management, and payout processing — no Stripe required.

🛰️ AI-POWERED OUTREACH
Auto-generate platform-specific content for each campaign. Post to Facebook groups, cross-platform publish, and track engagement — all from your command center.

⭐ COMMUNITY & TRANSPARENCY
Community groups, volunteer opportunities, institution grant applications, and full campaign transparency with donor updates and mission briefs.

🔒 SECURITY & COMPLIANCE
Campaign Protocol enforcement (P-1 through P-8), fraud control with PIN protection, admin approval workflows, and verified campaign badges.

Built with love for those who need support most. Every contribution gets us closer to orbit.
